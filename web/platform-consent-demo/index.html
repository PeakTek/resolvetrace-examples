Hit a bug? Record the session, review the clips, and submit only what you want. Nothing is uploaded until you press Submit.

Record an issue

Open Report / record an issue (bottom-right) and press Record. A red frame around the screen means you're recording. When you're happy, press Submit — only then do the clips upload; Discard throws them away.
